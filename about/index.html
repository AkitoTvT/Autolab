A BUSINESS WEBSITE CONCEPT

A place for cars.
And their next chapter.

AutoLab imagines a modern automotive business where discovering a car and looking after it belong in the same experience.

The collection brings model photography and dedicated vehicle pages together. The service center gives maintenance, detailing, and inspections their own space, with practical next steps.

Created by Marvin Miranda as a portfolio project and reusable website template. AutoLab is fictional; the design demonstrates how a real showroom or workshop could present its business online.
